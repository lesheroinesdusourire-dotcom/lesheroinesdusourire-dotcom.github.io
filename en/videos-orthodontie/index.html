The Smile Kingdom

Season 1 of the Smile Kingdom: a series of 24 videos of 30 seconds, online soon. The appliances introduce themselves.

Introduction

  • Welcome to the Smile Kingdom : The doctors welcome you to the Smile Kingdom.
  • Orthopaedics or orthodontics? : Jaws first, teeth next: the 2 stages of treatment.
  • At what age should you see an orthodontist? : From age 6, when the first permanent teeth come through.
  • Orthodontics for adults : Teeth move at any age: there is no age limit for a beautiful smile.

The appliances

  • Dixon, the palatal expander : It widens the palate, one screw turn at a time.
  • The plate : It enlarges the upper jaw.
  • The activator : It helps the lower jaw grow.
  • The PUL : It also helps the lower jaw catch up.
  • The face mask : It gently pulls the upper jaw forward.
  • The ELN : The night envelope that teaches the tongue to swallow well.
  • Max, the metal multi-bracket : The braces that straighten the teeth.
  • Max, ceramic version : The same braces, in tooth colour.
  • The aligner : Clear, it moves the teeth little by little.

The auxiliaries

  • The archwire : The wire that goes through the brackets and guides the teeth.
  • The elastics : They pull the teeth in the right direction.
  • The Carriere : It moves the back teeth backwards so the bite fits well.

The functions

  • Tongue posture : 17 muscles and one very precise place: up high, on the palate.
  • Swallowing : We swallow more than 2000 times a day. Here is the right method.
  • Breathe through the nose : Mouth closed, nose filtering the air: good for the jaw.
  • Chewing well : Small bites, on both sides, taking your time.

Recommendations

  • Brushing your teeth with braces : The technique for clean teeth around the brackets.
  • After an appointment : Sensitive teeth for a few days: this is normal.
  • Forbidden foods : Hard or sticky: what to avoid with braces.
  • Orthodontic wax : The magic little ball for when something rubs.
