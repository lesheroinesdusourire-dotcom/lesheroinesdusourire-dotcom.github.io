Elastics

With braces or aligners

What does it do?

Help the teeth go in the right direction.

When to wear it?

As stated on your sheet: day and/or night.

Remember

  • Wash your hands before putting them on or taking them off.
  • Change them after every meal and at bedtime (about 4 times a day).
  • No more elastics? Call us, we will give you more of the same strength.

Avoid

  • Never double the elastics: too much force damages the roots of the teeth

The golden rule

Change them often: they wear out quickly.
