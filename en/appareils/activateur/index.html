Activator

Removable appliance

What does it do?

Help the lower jaw grow.

When to wear it?

12 to 14 hours a day: all night + after school.

Remember

  • Wear it even more at the weekend.
  • The more you wear it, the faster the treatment goes.
  • For the first few hours, you produce a lot of saliva: this is normal.

Care

Morning and evening: a dedicated toothbrush and warm soapy water, then rinse. Dry it well before putting it away in its case.

The golden rule

In your mouth or in the case, never in a tissue.

Lost appliance: €300
