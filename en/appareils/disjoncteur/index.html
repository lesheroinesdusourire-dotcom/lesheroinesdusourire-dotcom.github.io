Palatal expander

Appliance fixed to the palate

What does it do?

Enlarge the upper jaw with a small screw.

When to wear it?

Activation: 1 turn a day.

Remember

  • Turn the key 1 time a day, in the direction of the arrow.
  • Pressure on the teeth after each turn is normal. A Doliprane can bring relief, follow the leaflet.
  • Very bad pain that doesn't go away? Call the practice.
  • A gap may appear between the 2 front teeth: this is normal.
  • You produce more saliva and lisp at first: talk a lot.

Care

Morning and evening: brush the appliance and the palate well (small interdental brush if food gets stuck) to prevent inflammation.

The golden rule

Appointment every week. Missed appointment = stop the key turns.
