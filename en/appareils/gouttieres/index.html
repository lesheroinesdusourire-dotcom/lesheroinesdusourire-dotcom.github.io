Aligners

Clear aligners

What does it do?

Straighten the teeth gently, aligner after aligner.

When to wear it?

22 hours out of 24, day and night.

Remember

  • You take them out only to eat and brush your teeth.
  • Brush your teeth before putting them back in.
  • Teeth are sensitive for 1 to 3 days with each new aligner: this is normal.
  • With elastics: never elastics without the aligner.

Care

At each brushing: toothbrush and warm soapy water.

The golden rule

Never elastics without the aligner.

Lost aligner: €80
