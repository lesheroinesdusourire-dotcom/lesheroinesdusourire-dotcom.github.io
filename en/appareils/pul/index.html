PUL

Removable appliance

What does it do?

Help the lower jaw grow.

When to wear it?

Day and night.

Remember

  • The first 2 days: after school and at night. After that, at school too.
  • Take it out to eat and to brush your teeth.
  • Take it out for the flute, the trumpet, swimming, judo or rugby.
  • It is normal for it to move if you open wide: the tongue holds it against the palate.
  • If there is an elastic: change it 2 times a week.
  • If there is a screw: 1 turn a week, in the direction of the arrow.

Care

Morning and evening: a dedicated toothbrush and warm soapy water, then rinse. Dry it well before putting it away in its case.

The golden rule

In your mouth or in the case, never in a tissue.
