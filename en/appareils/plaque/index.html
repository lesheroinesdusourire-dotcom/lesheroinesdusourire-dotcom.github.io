Plate

Removable appliance

What does it do?

Enlarge the upper jaw with a small screw.

When to wear it?

You keep it in even to eat.

Remember

  • Activation: 1 turn a week, in the direction of the arrow.
  • For the first few hours, you produce a lot of saliva: this is normal.
  • After a meal: take it out, rinse it and rinse your mouth.
  • You lisp at first: talk a lot or read aloud.
  • A small piece of resin coming off is normal wear. Mention it at the next appointment (unless it injures you).

Care

Morning and evening: a dedicated toothbrush and warm soapy water, then rinse. Dry it well before putting it away in its case.

The golden rule

In your mouth or in the case, never in a tissue.

Lost appliance: €300
