ELN

Nocturnal lingual envelope

What does it do?

Learn to swallow well.

When to wear it?

1 hour/day on weekdays, 2 hours at the weekend + every night.

Remember

  • Tongue up high, on the palate (not against the teeth).
  • Teeth closed together, upper teeth against lower teeth.
  • Lips at rest: they don't move when you swallow.
  • Practise drinking with small sips, with and then without the appliance, in front of a mirror.
  • Average duration: 6 months.

Care

Morning and evening: a dedicated toothbrush and warm soapy water, then rinse. Dry it well before putting it away in its case.

The golden rule

In your mouth or in the case, never in a tissue.

Lost appliance: €300
