Carriere

Bonded appliance + elastics

What does it do?

Move the upper back teeth backwards so the teeth fit together well.

When to wear it?

Elastics + aligner: 22 hours out of 24.

Remember

  • Wear elastics and aligner day and night, except to eat and brush your teeth.
  • Brush for 3 minutes after every meal, above and below the brackets.
  • Clean the aligner at each brushing: brush and warm soapy water.

Avoid

  • Chewing gum, caramel, hard or sticky sweets
  • Croutons and baguette sandwiches
  • Biting into hard foods: cut them into pieces
  • Chewing pens or biting your nails

Care

If a bracket comes loose, bring it to your next appointment.

The golden rule

Never elastics without the aligner.

Lost aligner: €80
