Braces

Fixed appliance

What does it do?

Straighten the teeth.

When to wear it?

Fixed permanently.

Remember

  • Brush for 3 minutes after every meal.
  • Brush the top and bottom separately, above and below the brackets.
  • With the tip of the brush, make small circles on each bracket, then brush from the gum towards the tooth.
  • After an appointment, your teeth are sensitive: a Doliprane can bring relief, follow the leaflet.

Avoid

  • Chewing gum, caramel, hard or sticky sweets
  • Croutons and baguette sandwiches (sliced bread is better)
  • Biting into an apple or a fruit with a stone: cut it into pieces
  • Chewing pens or biting your nails

Care

Chew gently with your back teeth.

The golden rule

A loose bracket? Bring it to your next appointment.

Lost bracket: €25
