Orthodontic emergencies: what to do if…

Most small problems can wait until the next working day.

A wire is poking my cheek

  • Cover the end of the wire with a small ball of orthodontic wax.
  • Or gently push it back with a pencil eraser.
  • Call the practice so we can shorten it.

A bracket has come loose

  • If it is still holding on the wire, leave it and put wax on it if it bothers you.
  • If it has fallen off, keep it in a box and bring it to your next appointment (a lost bracket is charged at €25).

I lost or broke my aligner

  • Put your previous aligner back in (always keep the old one!).
  • If the next one is ready and fits well, you can put it in.
  • Let the practice know quickly (a lost aligner is charged at €80).

I missed an appointment with a palatal expander

Stop the key turns until your next appointment, and call the practice.

My palatal expander hurts very badly

Call the practice.

I'm in pain

  • Sensitivity for a few days is normal.
  • Eat soft foods, have cool drinks.
  • A Doliprane can bring relief, follow the leaflet.
  • If the pain lasts more than a week, call us.

An appliance or palatal expander moves

Don't pull on it. Call the practice for a prompt appointment.

Real emergency

A blow to the teeth, a broken tooth, facial swelling or fever: call the practice. In the evening, at the weekend and on public holidays, dial the French on-call medical number 15 to reach the on-call dentist.
