The games

Free games to learn how to look after your appliance. The answers come from the practice's information sheets. Your progress stays on the phone, no account needed.

  • Today's challenge : 60 seconds, and a new challenge every day.
  • The Big Clean : Rub the teeth with your finger to remove the plaque.
  • The smart quiz : 10 random questions, explained by Léo and Max.
  • The right foods : Yes, in small pieces or no, depending on the appliance.
  • The puzzle : 9, 16 or 25 pieces to discover an appliance.
  • Tap the plaque! : Quickly touch the plaque patches.
  • Catch the right objects : Follow the mission: toothbrush, small interdental brush, aligner case…
  • True or false : Two big buttons and a short explanation.
  • 60 seconds on the clock : Mixed mini-challenges.
